Privacy Policy — FF Sensi Pack

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Android · com.ffsensipack.ff_sensi_pack

This policy explains what FF Sensi Pack (“the app”, “we”) does with information when you use it. The app is a sensitivity companion tool for Free Fire players.

The short version. The app collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no server. It makes no network requests at all, and works completely offline.

Information you enter

Everything you create in the app is stored locally on your device and never leaves it:

  • Saved sensitivity setups and their names
  • Your current sensitivity profile
  • Sensitivity history in the Tracker, including any notes you add
  • Your device profile answers (platform, performance, FPS, DPI, gyroscope)
  • Starred presets and your most recent analysis score

We cannot see this data, because it is never transmitted anywhere. It is removed when you use Settings → Clear Saved Data or uninstall the app.

No advertising or analytics

The app contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. It does not use your advertising identifier, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not build any profile of you.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, files, location or network.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Since it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Keeping and deleting data

All data stays on your device under your control. Clear it in the app’s settings, or uninstall the app to remove it entirely.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
